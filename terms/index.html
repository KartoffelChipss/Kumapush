2. What the Service does

KumaPush forwards alerts from your own Uptime Kuma installation to your iPhone. You register your device with the relay and get a personal webhook URL. When Uptime Kuma calls that URL, the relay converts the alert into a push notification and sends it to your device through Apple’s Push Notification service (APNs). See How it works for details.
